Biography

I am a Master student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (2026–2028). I am interested in how AI and mathematical modeling can help answer broad biological questions. My current work focuses on evaluation-first benchmarking of molecular prediction, mechanistic modeling of gene regulation and cellular perturbations, and mass spectrometry proteomics, from measurement to mechanism.

Previously, I obtained a B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics (2026) from Yangzhou University. I collaborate with Prof. Jun Xia (HKUST) and Chair Prof. Stan Z. Research Interests 研究兴趣

I work on evaluation-first machine learning for molecular and cellular biology: benchmarks that decide whether a score means anything, then models that survive them. Current directions: 我从事面向分子与细胞生物学的「评测优先」机器学习：先定义能判断分数是否有意义的基准，再构建经得起这些基准检验的模型。当前方向：

01 Evaluation-first benchmarking of molecular prediction 评测优先的分子预测基准

Benchmarks and evaluation protocols that test whether a score reflects real biology, not artefacts of the dataset, the metric, or the baselines. 构建基准与评测协议，检验分数反映的是真实生物学信号，而非数据集、指标或对比方法造成的假象。

02 Gene regulatory networks from multimodal physical evidence 基于多模态物理证据的基因调控网络

Using biological priors — sequence, interaction, structure — to constrain AI models trained on large-scale omics data. 以生物先验（序列、互作、结构）约束在大规模组学数据上训练的 AI 模型。

03 Cross-omics modeling along the Central Dogma 沿中心法则的跨组学建模

How RNA-level information becomes protein-level control, learned from multi-omics time courses. RNA 层面的信息如何转化为蛋白层面的调控：从多组学时间序列中学习。

De novo discovery of post-translational modifications, read directly from mass spectra. 从质谱谱图中直接发现新的翻译后修饰（de novo PTM discovery）。

04 Structure-aware recovery of proteomics measurements 结构感知的蛋白质组测量恢复

Low-rank and matrix-completion methods that fill gaps in molecular measurements without flattening the biology underneath. 用低秩与矩阵补全方法填补分子测量中的缺失，而不抹平其下的生物学结构。

Research Directions & Designs

Biophysically Constrained Directed Gene Networks for Perturbation Prediction

Traditional co-expression and statistical methods for Gene Regulatory Network (GRN) reconstruction often confuse simple co-activation with true biological regulation. This leads to models failing to generalize under zero-shot cellular perturbations (e.g., predicting the cell state after a novel gene knockout).

To address this, we developed VitaGRN, a deep learning framework that integrates multi-source physical evidence—including genomic sequences, chromatin states, protein-protein interactions (PPI), and 3D protein structures—into a biophysically constrained directed graph. By utilizing protein structural embeddings to guide neural perturbation predictions, VitaGRN transcends simple statistical correlations and achieves superior generalization.

Extensive evaluations demonstrate that VitaGRN outperforms 22 state-of-the-art network inference methods, establishing a zero-shot perturbation atlas across unseen cell lines.

read bioRxiv

Standardizing Proteomics MS/MS Spectrum Prediction & Benchmarking

Mass spectrometry (MS/MS) peptide spectrum prediction is a cornerstone of modern proteomics. However, existing machine learning models are often evaluated on disparate pipelines with severe sequence leakage, preventing fair comparisons and masking critical robustness gaps under distribution shifts.

We introduced PepSpecBench, a unified benchmarking platform that standardizes the evaluation of peptide MS/MS spectra predictors. By unifying six distinct model architectures in a high-dimensional representation space and enforcing sequence-disjoint train-test splits, PepSpecBench exposes key robustness vulnerabilities under physical perturbations (e.g., cross-species transitions and collision-energy shifts).

It provides the community with a standardized pipeline to drive the development of robust, generalizable biological machine learning models in proteomics.

Simulating Estuarine Dynamics and Microplastic Ecological Transport

Microplastics pose a growing threat to aquatic ecosystems. Traditional studies typically model toxicological impacts or hydrodynamic transport in isolation, failing to capture the physical-biological coupling in complex estuarine environments.

To bridge this gap, we developed a coupled hydrodynamic-ecological simulation framework on unstructured grids. The model integrates physical Lagrangian particle tracking and Eulerian diffusion with a biological trait-based ODE system.

Applied to the Yangtze River Estuary, it successfully mapped microplastic risk hotspots and demonstrated that wide, medium-exposure zones contribute to 66.6% of fish population declines. This work was awarded a China National Invention Patent (CN 119558222 B) for its coupled transport mesh algorithm.

物理与生物机理双重约束的基因调控网络及扰动预测

传统的基因调控网络（GRN）重构方法常混淆共激活（同源基因级联）与真实的生物物理调控，导致模型在零样本细胞扰动任务（如预测新基因敲除后的细胞状态）中缺乏泛化能力。

为此，我们开发了 VitaGRN 深度学习框架。该框架将基因组序列、染色质状态、蛋白质相互作用（PPI）及三维蛋白质结构等多源物理证据有机融入双重约束的有向图中。通过利用蛋白质结构嵌入来指导细胞扰动预测，VitaGRN 摆脱了简单的统计相关性，实现了更强的机理可解释性。

在有向网络推理与扰动基准测试中，VitaGRN 击败了 22 种主流算法，并在未见过的细胞系上构建了零样本扰动图谱。

PepSpecBench：肽链质谱图谱预测与模型评估标准化基准

质谱图谱（MS/MS）肽链预测是现代蛋白质组学的重要基石。然而，现有的机器学习模型大多在碎片化的测试流程上进行评估，且存在严重的序列泄漏问题，无法进行公平的模型比对，也掩盖了分布偏移下的鲁棒性隐患。

我们推出了 PepSpecBench 统一评测基准平台，以规范肽链质谱预测模型的评估。该平台在统一的高维空间中重构了 6 种主流模型，实施了序列不交集的划分（Sequence-disjoint splits），并引入了跨物种和碰撞能量偏移等物理扰动测试。

它为蛋白质组学机器学习模型的科学进步提供了标准基准，揭示了现有模型在物理扰动下的鲁棒性缺陷。

非结构网格下河口动力学仿真与微塑料生态传输模拟

微塑料对水生生态系统构成了日益严重的威胁。传统研究通常孤立地模拟毒理学影响或水动力输运，未能捕捉到复杂河口环境中物理与生物的耦合动力学反馈。

为了填补这一空白，我们开发了非结构网格下的水动力-生态耦合仿真框架。该模型将拉格朗日粒子追踪（物理输运）和欧拉扩散与基于特性的生物响应微分方程相结合。

在长江口的应用表明，该模型成功绘制了微塑料污染的风险热点，并证明中等暴露强度的宽阔水域导致了 66.6% 的鱼类种群衰退。该工作凭借其耦合非结构网格传输算法，荣获了中国国家发明专利授权（CN 119558222 B）。
